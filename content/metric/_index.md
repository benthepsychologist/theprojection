title: "The metrics"
---

Every number on the board is a **claim** — a value we assert about an actor,
with the sources behind it one click away. These pages are the other half of
that contract: **what each metric means and how it is calculated**, so the
recipe is as inspectable as the figures.

Four measured axes, plus three supporting reads. None of this is novel — each
axis deliberately adopts an established formalization from finance,
economics, or power-measurement literature, named on its page. Where our
figure is an estimate, the claim says so; a metric with no visible source is
a bug.
