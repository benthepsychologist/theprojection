## 2026-07-23 — Tankers struck, blockade declared

- **Houthi (Iran-backed) forces struck two Saudi oil tankers** (*Encelia*,
  *Layla*) in the Red Sea and declared a naval blockade of Saudi ports —
  the first direct tanker strikes of the current conflict; Trump
  threatened to hold Iran responsible and floated bombing Iranian
  infrastructure.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/iran-war-us-trump-houthis-red-sea-oil.html)) ⟨daily 2026-07-23⟩
- **Brent crude closed +7% at ~$100.69/bbl** (intraday peak ~$102),
  highest in 8 weeks — Goldman flagged a path to $120+ if disruption
  persists. Became the proximate driver of the day's Treasury-yield jump,
  Fed-hike-odds spike, and equity selloff — compounded the Alphabet-led
  Magnificent 7 rout into its worst day since April 2025's tariff
  tantrum.
  ([NBC/Yahoo](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/oil-prices-rise-red-sea-attacks-houthis-saudi-trump-iran-war-rcna588851)) ⟨daily 2026-07-23⟩

## 2026-07-24 — Partial retreat

- **Brent retreated ~4.3% to ~$97.04**, pulling back from the peak as new
  Trump tariffs (60 countries, effective 07-24) added a competing
  demand-destruction headwind.
  ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-july-24)) ⟨daily 2026-07-24⟩
