## 2026-07-28 — Brent craters as the wider war pauses

- **Brent fell 4.8% to ~$84.09**, a third straight losing session — well
  below even the 07-24 retreat level — as the US-Iran fighting pause held
  through Tuesday and Trump cited "good talks" with Tehran and a "good
  chance" of a resolution (while warning strikes resume if diplomacy
  fails). Oil is now a disinflationary story again, cutting against one
  of the week's own drivers of Fed hike odds.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/oil-price-today-wti-brent-us-iran-hormuz.html)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩

## 2026-07-26/27 — Bab el-Mandeb traffic collapses; the shooting war itself quietly pauses

- **Only 11 tankers transited Bab el-Mandeb on Sunday** — a multi-month
  low (Kpler data via Reuters) — as Saudi and Western operators reroute
  north to the Suez Canal or turn back rather than run the Houthi
  blockade. The price shock partly cooled but the physical disruption is
  now showing up in shipping data, not just spot prices.
  ([Japan Times](https://www.japantimes.co.jp/news/2026/07/27/world/red-sea-shipping-houthi-saudi-arabia/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩
- **Separately: the US quietly halted strikes on Iran late Friday
  (07-24) and Iran ceased retaliatory strikes** — a broader US-Iran
  fighting pause, distinct from (and not yet extended to) the ongoing
  Houthi Red Sea campaign against Saudi shipping and infrastructure.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/oil-price-wti-brent-slide-as-iran-reportedly-may-halt-attacks.html)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩

## 2026-07-25 — Direct hit: first Saudi oil-infrastructure strike since 2022

- **Houthi drones struck Aramco refineries at Jizan and Yanbu** — Yanbu
  alone handles ~92% of Saudi seaborne crude exports (Kpler/AFP) — the
  first direct strike on Saudi oil infrastructure since 2022; Brent spiked
  back above $100/bbl same day.
  ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/07/25/houthis-claim-attack-saudi-oil-refinery-iran-war-widens-red-sea/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩

## 2026-07-23 — Tankers struck, blockade declared

- **Houthi (Iran-backed) forces struck two Saudi oil tankers** (*Encelia*,
  *Layla*) in the Red Sea and declared a naval blockade of Saudi ports —
  the first direct tanker strikes of the current conflict; Trump
  threatened to hold Iran responsible and floated bombing Iranian
  infrastructure.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/23/iran-war-us-trump-houthis-red-sea-oil.html)) ⟨daily 2026-07-23⟩
- **Brent crude closed +7% at ~$100.69/bbl** (intraday peak ~$102),
  highest in 8 weeks — Goldman flagged a path to $120+ if disruption
  persists. Became the proximate driver of the day's Treasury-yield jump,
  Fed-hike-odds spike, and equity selloff — compounded the Alphabet-led
  Magnificent 7 rout into its worst day since April 2025's tariff
  tantrum.
  ([NBC/Yahoo](https://www.nbcnews.com/business/markets/oil-prices-rise-red-sea-attacks-houthis-saudi-trump-iran-war-rcna588851)) ⟨daily 2026-07-23⟩

## 2026-07-24 — Partial retreat

- **Brent retreated ~4.3% to ~$97.04**, pulling back from the peak as new
  Trump tariffs (60 countries, effective 07-24) added a competing
  demand-destruction headwind.
  ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-23/latest-oil-market-news-and-analysis-for-july-24)) ⟨daily 2026-07-24⟩
