## 2026-07-30 — Diplomacy fragments; no ceasefire, no new combatant confirmed

  protect Red Sea shipping from Houthi attacks** — its first organized
  multilateral security response to the blockade, reported consistently
  across three outlets (Al-Monitor, Jerusalem Post, Al Jazeera), all
  citing sources, 07-29 evening. ⟨daily 2026-07-30⟩

## 2026-07-29/30 — The war widened past the triggering strike

- **The US struck targets inside Iran directly** — Bandar Abbas, Kish
  Island, and sites across Khuzestan's oil belt — killing civilians on
  Qeshm Island in a residential building hit.
  ([NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/07/30/nx-s1-5913077)) ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩
- **Saudi Arabia joined US airstrikes against Iran-backed militias in
  Iraq** — its first direct military action of this kind, reported
  ~20 PMF fighters killed (one source adds 6 Iranian advisers), 32
  wounded; stated cause was 30+ drone attacks on US/Saudi positions over
  the prior 72 hours.
  ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/where/iraq/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩
- **Two new countries were hit — Kuwait and Egypt.** An Iranian strike
  killed a worker at a Chinese-run site in northern Kuwait; a drone hit
  two vessels (Gaslog Salem, Energos Winter) at Egypt's Damietta port,
  reported as the first attack on Egyptian soil in this war.
  ([Kuwait Times](https://www.kuwaittimes.com/) · [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩

## 2026-07-28 — The pause breaks

- **Iran fired ballistic missiles at a US air base and a CENTCOM
  facility in Jordan at 5:45pm ET** — all intercepted, no casualties.
  CENTCOM called it "an attempted surprise attack." The first Iranian
  ballistic-missile attack on a US base since the 07-24 pause — the
  event that triggered the flash rail.
  ([Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/07/28/iran-missile-attack-us-base-jordan)
  · [CNN](https://edition.cnn.com/2026/07/28/world/live-news/iran-trump-news)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩
- **Earlier the same day, the pause still appeared to hold** — Trump
  cited "good talks" with Tehran and a "good chance" of a resolution,
  while warning strikes resume if diplomacy fails. Both were true at
  16:00 ET and false by 17:45 ET — **the pause is a headline, not a
  state**, broken once within four days of being declared.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/28/oil-price-today-wti-brent-us-iran-hormuz.html)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩
- **The war's other front got photographed:** satellite imagery
  corroborated Iranian strikes on two Amazon data centres in Bahrain
  (Zallaq, Askar) — claimed ~07-21, following earlier drone attacks on
  AWS sites in the UAE (03-01) and Bahrain (04-01); Iranian state media
  had named Microsoft, Google, Apple, Meta, Oracle, Intel and Nvidia as
  targets on 03-31. This war now runs through compute infrastructure
  directly — full capex/AI-lens read in `aws-capex` and
  `datacenters-as-targets`.
  ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-28/amazon-data-centers-hit-in-iran-strikes-satellite-images-show)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩

## 2026-07-26/27 — The shooting war itself quietly pauses

- **The US quietly halted strikes on Iran late Friday (07-24) and Iran
  ceased retaliatory strikes** — a broader US-Iran fighting pause,
  distinct from (and not yet extended to) the ongoing Houthi Red Sea
  campaign against Saudi shipping and infrastructure.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/27/oil-price-wti-brent-slide-as-iran-reportedly-may-halt-attacks.html)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩

## 2026-07-25 — Direct hit: first Saudi oil-infrastructure strike since 2022

- **Houthi drones struck Aramco refineries at Jizan and Yanbu** — Yanbu
  alone handles ~92% of Saudi seaborne crude exports (Kpler/AFP) — the
  first direct strike on Saudi oil infrastructure since 2022. Market
  reaction (Brent spiked above $100/bbl same day) tracked on
  `red-sea-oil-shock`.
  ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/07/25/houthis-claim-attack-saudi-oil-refinery-iran-war-widens-red-sea/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩

## 2026-07-23 — Tankers struck, blockade declared

- **Houthi (Iran-backed) forces struck two Saudi oil tankers** (*Encelia*,
  *Layla*) in the Red Sea and declared a naval blockade of Saudi ports —
  the first direct tanker strikes of the current conflict; Trump
  threatened to hold Iran responsible and floated bombing Iranian
  infrastructure. The originating event of this war — market reaction
  (Brent +7% to ~$100.69, the day's equity selloff) tracked on
  `red-sea-oil-shock`.
