## 2026-07-20/21 — Complaint + hearing (the week the digest caught)

- **NUHW filed a 29-page formal complaint to DMHC + US DOL** — the
  "E-Visit" auto-triage tool and unlicensed TSR phone triage as illegal
  unlicensed triage; instant unreviewed AI referrals documented with
  dated screenshots.
  ([complaint PDF](https://nuhw.org/wp-content/uploads/NUHW-KaiserNorCalE-VisitComplaint.7.20.2026.pdf)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-22⟩
- **The SF hearing was a formal Committee of the Whole** (File 260747,
  Sup. Chen; clinician + expert panels; Kaiser declined to present;
  procedural "heard and filed").
  ([SF Board agenda](https://media.api.sf.gov/documents/bag072126_agenda.pdf)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-22⟩

## 2026-03-18 — Behavioral health's first union strike over AI

- **~2,400 clinicians struck for one day** (92% authorization; ~23k CNA
  nurses in solidarity) on the ULP charge over Kaiser's unilateral triage
  overhaul — days after Kaiser's $31M DOL parity settlement (02-10).
  ([NUHW](https://home.nuhw.org/2026/03/23/mental-health-clinicians-hold-major-strike-with-support-from-kaiser-permanente-nurses-engineers/)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-22⟩
- **Bargaining then stalled on the "Terrible Three"** — AI replacement at
  will, outsourcing, "sole discretion" over clinical decisions; Kaiser's
  06-04 counter was a non-binding quarterly AI committee ("toothless" —
  NUHW). Mediation set for ~August. ⟨crawl 2026-07-22⟩

## 2023→2025 — The systems and the rap sheet

- **The AI at issue is specific:** late-2023 "E-Visit" questionnaire
  (partial PHQ-9/GAD-7) auto-generating referrals + Jan-2024 unlicensed
  TSR phone triage — replacing licensed triage clinicians.
  ([KQED](https://www.kqed.org/science/1999553)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-22⟩
- **Regulators were already on the field:** $200M DMHC settlement (2023);
  DMHC's 02-2025 survey citing failed suicide-risk screening in triage;
  Kaiser signed "AI will assist, not replace" with its SoCal union then
  deleted it from the NorCal proposal.
  ([DMHC survey](https://www.dmhc.ca.gov/desktopmodules/dmhc/medsurveys/surveys/055_nr_full%20service_022525.pdf)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-22⟩
- **The legislative echo:** NUHW-co-sponsored **SB 903** (bar AI-only
  screening by health plans) passed the CA Senate — cross-ref
  `state-therapy-chatbot-bans`. ⟨crawl 2026-07-22⟩
