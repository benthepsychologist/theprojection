## 2026-07-22 — Primary source pinned: DOE names the first 278 awards

- **Energy Secretary Chris Wright announced the first Genesis Mission
  project selections** — 278 awards (87 DOE/NNSA National Labs, 168
  universities, 19 companies, 4 nonprofits) across 342 participating
  institutions, plus **$800M+ in named partner commitments** (Microsoft
  $60M, IBM $50M quantum access, Google $40M in AI resources, plus HPE,
  Wiley, OpenAI). This is the actual program document the thread opened
  looking for — official energy.gov releases, not press paraphrase.
  Awardees get the "Genesis Mission Platform": AI agent frameworks, lab
  compute, and industry-partner model access. Focus areas: materials
  discovery, nuclear energy, quantum, climate/weather, accelerator
  research — the nuclear-for-AI overlap the thread is watching for.
  ([DOE](https://www.energy.gov/articles/secretary-energy-chris-wright-announces-first-genesis-mission-projects-selected-accelerate) ·
