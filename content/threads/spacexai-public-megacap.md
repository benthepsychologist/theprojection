  Breaking Defense, Aviation Week), but SPCX still fell ~3.3% on the day,
  underscoring Morgan Stanley's "AI business priced at zero" read from
  07-28: government launch revenue isn't moving the stock either.

## 2026-07-28 — Congress buys the dip as Morgan Stanley prices the AI unit at zero

- **SPCX touched $110.85, ~50% off its post-IPO peak** — Morgan Stanley's
  Adam Jonas says a further drop to $100 means the market is pricing
  SpaceX's AI business (Grok/Cursor/xAI) at literally zero; he still
  holds a $300 target (>50% of it from AI) and calls the disconnect a
  buying opportunity — ~80% of covering analysts remain Buy-rated,
  average target >$230.
  ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/eriksherman/2026/07/26/spacex-is-15-away-from-a-worthless-ai-business-says-morgan-stanley/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩
- **Six members of Congress bought SPCX stock post-IPO** ($182K-$480K
  combined, 5 Republicans + 1 Democrat) — including Rep. Lisa McClain
  (House GOP leadership) and Rep. John James, both on committees touching
  SpaceX's defense/satellite/AI/federal-contracting business. A fresh
  conflict-of-interest angle riding alongside the price story.
  ([24/7 Wall St.](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/07/28/congressman-who-oversees-military-contracts-bought-100000-in-spacex-stock-hes-not-the-only-one/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-28⟩

## 2026-07-23 — Below the IPO price

## 2026-07-07 — Forced into the Nasdaq-100

- **Added to the Nasdaq-100** ~15 trading days post-IPO, forcing ~$4.3B of
  passive buying onto a ~3–4% float — passive holders now carry a
  money-losing AI mega-cap.
  ([Nasdaq IR](https://ir.nasdaq.com/news-releases/news-release-details/space-exploration-technologies-corporation-join-nasdaq-100)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-24⟩

## 2026-06-12→16 — The record IPO, then the peak

- **IPO June 12: 555.5M Class-A shares @ $135 on Nasdaq (SPCX), ~$75B raised
  (largest ever), ~$1.75T valuation**; opened $160.95, closed ~$161 (+19%).
  Peaked ~$225 / ~$2.6T on June 16. (Corrects our "~$2.1T ~June" seed — that
  conflated the debut close and the peak.)
  ([SEC 424B4](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026042639/)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-24⟩

## 2026-05-20 — The S-1 opens the books

- **S-1 filed**: FY2025 consolidated revenue $18.67B, operating loss $2.59B;
  the money map is **Starlink as the cash engine** (~$4.4B FY25 op income,
  39% margin) funding **Starship R&D + xAI's Colossus burn** — xAI burned
  $7.7B of capex in Q1-26 alone, dropping cash $24.7B→$15.9B in a quarter.
  The IPO is essentially the financing bridge for the AI gap.
  ([SEC S-1](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1181412/000162828026036936/spaceexplorationtechnologi.htm)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-24⟩

## 2026-02-02 — The $1.25T merger

- **SpaceX acquired xAI all-stock** (xAI $250B; combined $1.25T) — xAI
  becomes a wholly-owned subsidiary; the largest private merger ever.
  Backstory: xAI had itself acquired X/Twitter in March 2025.
  ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/02/03/musk-xai-spacex-biggest-merger-ever.html)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-24⟩

## Control & the open questions

- **Musk holds ~42% economically but ~82–85% of the vote** via Class-B
  supervoting, with a non-voting Class-C authorized to raise capital without
  diluting control — concentration extreme enough that CBS floated him as
  the first trillionaire. Open: is the Anthropic compute lease-back durable
  revenue or circular AI-financing (numbers conflict, $1.25B/mo vs
  $6.3B/yr)? Does the Aug-6 lock-up break the tiny-float dynamic?
