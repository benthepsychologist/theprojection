## 2026-07-29 — Earnings beat, Azure accelerates, the named split still didn't happen

- **Microsoft reports FY26 Q4 after today's close**, confirmed against
  two Microsoft primary sources; the watch items are the FY27 capex
  guide and any OpenAI-vs-own split disclosure.
  ([Microsoft](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/07/08/microsoft-announces-quarterly-earnings-release-date-68/)) ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩
- **Record the date churn plainly:** this thread's prose said 07-29, the
  expectations ledger said 07-30, the ledger was trusted and the prose
  "corrected" — then Microsoft's own IR settled it at 07-29. The ledger
  was the wrong authority. ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩
- **Revenue $90.0B (+18%) and EPS $4.81 (+32%) both beat, and Azure grew
  43% constant-currency** — well above the 39-40% guide, and an
  acceleration from the prior quarter. Stock +8-9% after-hours.
  ([Microsoft IR](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q4/press-release-webcast)) ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩
- **⚠ Quality-of-beat flag:** the EPS figure includes a ~$3.2B unrealized
  mark-to-market gain on Microsoft's Anthropic stake. The operating beat
  is real but smaller than the headline number.
  ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/earnings/26/07/60786971/microsoft-stock-ai-capex-amy-hood-gpu-spending-surges-after-hours)) ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩
- **The named "first" — an OpenAI-vs-own capex split — did NOT happen.**
  Checked the call transcript directly: the only OpenAI-adjacent
  disclosure was commercial RPO growing 25% excluding OpenAI, plus a
  reiteration of OpenAI's $250B Azure spend commitment. Neither is a
  split. FY27 capex guidance stayed qualitative — "will grow
  year-over-year," next quarter guided "over $50B" — and the ~$255-260B
  full-year figure circulating in preview coverage was NOT stated on the
  call; treat it as analyst preview, not company guidance.
  ([transcript via Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-microsoft-q4-2026-beats-forecasts-stock-jumps-8-93CH-4822020)) ⟨daily 2026-07-29⟩

- **Sites: the "AI superfactory" linking Wisconsin and Georgia** — Mount
  Pleasant, WI Fairwater #1 finished construction 2026-06-23 (second
  facility slated 2028); a second Fairwater site near Atlanta, GA,
  unveiled 2025-11-12, is wired to it via 120,000+ miles of dedicated
  fiber, forming Microsoft's first named "AI superfactory," reported at
  "over 2GW" combined capacity.
  ([Microsoft Source](https://news.microsoft.com/source/2026/06/23/microsoft-completes-construction-on-first-datacenter-facility-in-mount-pleasant-wisconsin/)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-27⟩
- **Sites: a second, separate 2GW site in Texas** — Pecos, Reeves County
  TX announced 2026-06-22, ~2GW, co-located with a Chevron-built
  natural-gas plant ("Project Kilby," $7B, 20-year PPA, first power
  2028) — a distinct 2GW figure from the WI+GA superfactory, not to be
  summed with it without more confirmation; no single company-wide
  total-GW figure exists anywhere in the public record.
  ([Microsoft blog](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/06/22/powering-the-next-wave-of-ai-expanding-capacity-with-our-new-datacenter-in-pecos/)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-27⟩
- **Silicon: Maia 200 is explicitly dual-purpose** — launched 2026-01-26
  (TSMC 3nm, 140B transistors), deployed to serve both OpenAI's GPT-5.2
  inference and Microsoft's own in-house model training/RL — Microsoft's
  own post confirms the split exists but gives no percentage; no
  production-volume figure has been disclosed anywhere.
  ([Microsoft blog](https://blogs.microsoft.com/blog/2026/01/26/maia-200/)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-27⟩
- **Power: Three Mile Island/Crane restart advancing toward 2027** — DOE
  approved a $1B loan to Constellation (2025-11-18); NRC issued a draft
  finding of no significant environmental impact and FERC approved 760MW
  of interconnection rights (2026-06), with operators affirming a 2027
  restart date.
  ([E&E News via Google News RSS](https://news.google.com/rss/search?q=Microsoft%20nuclear%20power%20purchase%20agreement%202026&hl=en-US&gl=US&ceid=US:en)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-27⟩
- **Payoff: the $37B AI run-rate and the un-mapped $250B OpenAI
  commitment** — Microsoft's FY26 Q3 (2026-04-29) reported AI run-rate at
  $37B annualized (+123% YoY), Azure +40% YoY, and nine-month FY26 capex
  of $80.1B; the October 2025 OpenAI deal set a $250B incremental Azure
  purchase commitment with no disclosed timeframe, and no source
  anywhere quantifies what share of Microsoft's capex serves that
  commitment versus Microsoft's own workloads — the central open
  question the earnings call (07-29) may or may not address.
  ([Microsoft IR](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/investor/earnings/fy-2026-q3/press-release-webcast)) ⟨crawl 2026-07-27⟩
